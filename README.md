## ▌WELCOME TO THE ARCADE

Some games give you a story. Some give you a world. And some give you one suspiciously attractive rival, a 400-year-old vampire, twelve secret organizations and enough unanswered questions to keep a wiki tab open until 3 AM.

**LoreHub is for all of it.**

This is a fan-made gaming wiki and archive built for people who love getting lost in game universes. Explore characters, factions, locations, events, rivalries and the little details that make a fictional world feel bigger than the screen.

No sweaty leaderboard energy. No pretending every game needs a competitive meta. Just games, lore and the joy of being a massive nerd about them.

> **THE HOUSE RULE:** If it's worth pausing the game to look up, it's worth a place in the archive.

## ▌CHOOSE A WORLD

LoreHub currently features six game-world archives. Pick a universe and start digging.

### `WORLD 01` — MEGA MAN ZERO
Zero, Ciel's Resistance, Neo Arcadia and a future that needs saving. Reploids, rebellion and a whole lot of sword business.

### `WORLD 02` — TEKKEN
The Mishima bloodline, the Devil Gene, tournament history and rivalries where the family group chat would be a war crime against peace and quiet.

### `WORLD 03` — RESIDENT EVIL
Outbreaks, biohazards, secret experiments, Umbrella's endless mess, and familiar faces including Leon, Claire and Ada.

### `WORLD 04` — THE KING OF FIGHTERS
Kyo and Iori's rivalry, the Orochi Saga, tournament history and fighters who make settling things peacefully look deeply overrated.

### `WORLD 05` — CASTLEVANIA
Dracula, the Belmont legacy, Shanoa, Ecclesia and the glyphs behind *Order of Ecclesia*. Bring a whip. Maybe a therapist.

### `WORLD 06` — FINAL FANTASY
A home for distinct numbered worlds. The current collection includes *Final Fantasy V* and its Crystals, party and Job System, alongside a separate *Final Fantasy VII* entry featuring Cloud Strife.

## ▌WHAT'S IN THE ARCHIVE?

- **Characters** — heroes, villains, rivals, weirdos and scene-stealers.
- **Factions** — secret societies, armies, clans, corporations and other people with questionable plans.
- **Locations** — castles, cities, laboratories, battlefields and places you absolutely should not enter alone.
- **Events & story arcs** — the big moments, the turning points and the lore that connects them.
- **Related entries** — follow the trail when one character, event or faction leads to another.
- **Saved lore** — keep the pages you want to revisit close at hand.

The collection is a growing fan archive, not a claim to contain every fact about every franchise. There's always another entry waiting to be written.

## ▌WHY THIS EXISTS

Because a search result isn't always the same thing as exploring a world.

Sometimes you want to trace a rivalry from its beginning. Sometimes you want to know why a character matters, how a faction fits into the story, or what happened in that one game you played years ago. Sometimes you just want to fall into a wiki rabbit hole because the soundtrack is still playing in your head.

**LoreHub exists to make that rabbit hole a fun place to visit.**

It's a little love letter to gaming, built one entry at a time. More worlds, more characters, more lore and more reasons to say *"just one more page."*
