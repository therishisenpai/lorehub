# LOREHUB
### GAMES · WORLDS · PEAK

**Explore peak. Revisit the classics. Get lost in the lore.**

LoreHub is a fan-made corner of the internet for people who get a little too invested in video games.

The rivalries. The transformations. The ridiculous move lists. The mysterious organizations. The final bosses who refuse to stay down. The tiny bits of lore you only discover after falling down a three-hour wiki rabbit hole.

Whether you came for an arcade grudge match, a 2D action classic, a crystal-powered RPG adventure or a survival-horror nightmare, there's always another rabbit hole waiting.

## WHAT'S ON THE ARCADE FLOOR?

A hand-picked shelf of games from the eras of chunky sprites, unforgettable soundtracks, dramatic boss fights and manuals you actually wanted to read.

- **The King of Fighters** — dream teams, rivalries and the Orochi Saga.
- **Castlevania** — gothic castles, dangerous corridors and glyph-powered combat.
- **Mega Man Zero** — fast swordplay, brutal missions and a future that needs saving.
- **Final Fantasy V** — crystals, airships and a job system with a build for every mood.
- **Tekken** — family drama, questionable parenting and the King of Iron Fist Tournament.
- **Resident Evil** — locked doors, suspicious laboratories and absolutely not enough ammunition.

This shelf is a love letter to games that make one more continue feel inevitable.

## STEP INTO THE WORLDS

LoreHub currently has six game-world shelves:

### MEGA MAN ZERO
Zero, Ciel's Resistance, Neo Arcadia and a future that needs saving.

### TEKKEN
The Mishima bloodline, the Devil Gene, rivalries and tournaments where family therapy clearly failed.

### RESIDENT EVIL
Outbreaks, biohazards, secret experiments, Umbrella's mess, Leon, Claire and Ada.

### THE KING OF FIGHTERS
Kyo and Iori's rivalry, the Orochi Saga, tournament history and fighters like Blue Mary.

### CASTLEVANIA
Dracula, the Belmont legacy, Shanoa, Ecclesia and the glyphs that power Order of Ecclesia.

### FINAL FANTASY
A franchise shelf for distinct numbered worlds. The current collection covers Final Fantasy V's Crystals, party and Job System, plus a clearly separate Final Fantasy VII entry for Cloud Strife.

Browse characters, factions, locations, events and concepts. Follow related entries. Save the pages you want to come back to.

## WHY LOREHUB EXISTS

Because sometimes you want more than a quick search result.

You want to remember who that boss was, understand why two rivals hate each other, trace a faction's history, revisit a game's world, or settle an argument that absolutely did not need to become a 40-message group chat.

LoreHub is meant to make that kind of exploring feel fun, clear and easy to jump into. It's a small fan archive with room to grow: more worlds, more games, more characters and more lore rabbit holes.

## A QUICK NOTE

LoreHub is an early, fan-made prototype. Its collection is a curated sample rather than a complete encyclopedia. Saved lore and submitted ideas stay in the browser where they were added; they are not yet shared with other visitors or published to a central community database.

To take a look, open **`index.html`** in a modern web browser.
